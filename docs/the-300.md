# The 300: green within walking distance

Does everyone live within 300 m of a park or wood?

The 300 is measured as walking time over the street and path network, starting from the entrances of the green. A home within a 5-minute walk meets the rule. The 15-minute zone is computed too, for information.

## How it is made (FME, PostGIS and Valhalla, for now)

The workbench [`indicator_300_park/fme/300_2025 regel.fmw`](https://github.com/RobertoTjesse/3-30-300-regel/tree/master/indicator_300_park) writes `300.gdb`:

1. Parks and woods from OpenStreetMap and TOP10NL are merged (2024).
2. Only green of at least 300 m² with a perimeter/area ratio of at most 0.35 is kept. The ratio removes long narrow strips such as verges: a strip has to be about 6 m wide or more.
3. Every walkable OSM way within 300 m of a municipality is intersected with the edge of the green. Where a path enters the green is an entrance, 27,111 in total.
4. From every entrance, Valhalla (pedestrian profile, 5.1 km/h) computes the area you can reach in 5 and in 15 minutes, and all zones are dissolved.
5. A BAG pand with a woonfunctie that lies in or touches a 5-minute zone meets the rule.

`web/build_tiles_30_300.py` then counts, per gemeente, wijk and buurt, the share of homes within 5 and within 15 minutes, weighted by the number of homes per building. The green from steps 1 and 2 is on the map as well. The FME results only hold the entrances, so `web/build_tiles_groen.py` reads the workbench's input `groenkaart.gdb` with the same selection: it leaves out TOP10NL water, as the workbench's reader does, and applies the filter of step 2.

## Choices

| Choice | Why |
|---|---|
| Walking time over the network | 300 m as the crow flies can mean a long detour around water, a railway or a motorway. The paper itself says "about a 5-minute walk". |
| 5 minutes | About 425 m over the network, which with the usual detour factor is about 300 m as the crow flies. |
| From the entrances | You enter a park by a path. Measuring from the edge would count fences, water and dense edges as access. |
| At least 300 m², not too narrow | Small plantings and verges are not places to spend time. |
| OSM and TOP10NL | Each misses parts: OSM is strong on parks and paths, TOP10NL on woods and land outside town. |
| A building counts if it touches the zone | The front door is somewhere on the facade. |

## Known issues

- 300 m² is far below the WHO's 1 ha. The paper and the WHO mean a large public green space, while here a small park counts too, so results are more favourable than under the strict norm. A run with 1 ha is the first improvement.
- Whether the green is publicly accessible is not checked.
- Green without paths, such as an open lawn by the street, gets no entrance and does not count.
- The BAG used here (2025) differs from the 3's (2026), so home counts per area can differ slightly.
